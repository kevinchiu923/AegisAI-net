Common questions

Do I need to be technical to use it?

No. Assessment runs as a guided questionnaire with context on every item; the output is lights and scores, not code.

Where does the data live?

The platform can be fully self-hosted in your or your client's environment — assessment data never leaves your infrastructure.

How is a partnership priced?

It depends on the model — white-label licensing, per-engagement splits, or internal licensing. Reach out on LinkedIn and we'll talk through your case.

How fast can we start?

The platform runs today, with sixteen standards and demonstration data built in. A pilot assessment typically completes within weeks.

Does AegisAI cover the EU AI Act?

AegisAI ships with an EU AI Act reference knowledge base: obligation summaries for the key articles, the Annex III high-risk areas, and an Omnibus-aware application timeline (high-risk obligations apply from 2 December 2027). It is a reference view to structure your assessment — not legal advice.

What happens if we haven't finished the assessment?

AegisAI returns Insufficient Evidence, not No-Go. The launch is still blocked — we cannot vouch for a mandatory control nobody has assessed — but the report says the reason is missing evidence, not a deficiency we found. The fix is to finish the questionnaire, not to rebuild a control that may be perfectly fine.

Can AegisAI tell us which AI system to spend the budget on?

Yes. Alongside exposure and control gaps, AegisAI classifies consequence — how bad a failure would be — from five checkable declarations: people affected, safety of life, regulated use, reversibility, and financial exposure. The briefing plots exposure against consequence and carries the gap index in each cell, surfacing the systems a single risk score hides. Consequence is customer-declared, so it feeds no score, no finding, and no verdict.

Can AegisAI measure our AI governance maturity?

Yes — AegisAI includes the official OWASP AIMA v1.0 question bank (144 questions across 8 practice areas) and derives a per-area maturity estimate from your control assessments. The derived score is an initial estimate, not a formal AIMA self-assessment.
