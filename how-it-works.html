How it works

Three steps. One defensible verdict.

No code to write, no models to understand — a guided process any team can run.

The process

The three steps

Step 1 · Register

Register the AI system

Answer basic questions about the system: purpose, data, exposure. A risk tier is calculated automatically.

It answers: what AI are your clients actually running?

Step 2 · Assess

Guided questionnaire

Only the controls relevant to that system appear, each with context and space for evidence uploads — plus a derived AIMA maturity estimate per practice area (an initial estimate, not a formal self-assessment).

It answers: how mature are the controls, really?

Step 3 · Report

Get the governance report

Traffic lights, gap scores, remediation guidance — formatted for boards, not engineers.

It answers: what should be fixed first?

Combined into

Insufficient Evidence · Go · Conditional · No-Go

Every system gets one clear deployment verdict — with the full evidence trail behind it. Two of the four block a launch, and they say different things: No-Go means we assessed a mandatory control and it failed. Insufficient Evidence means we never got to look at it. Same block, opposite fix.

Methodology

How is the verdict calculated?

1 · Measure

Every applicable control gets a maturity score from 0 to 5. Compliance, partial implementation and assessment progress are reported as three separate numbers — a half-built control is never counted as a finished one.

2 · Weigh

Each gap is weighted, and every weight carries a documented source and rationale. Weights are tunable with an audit trail, and a backtest module can validate them against your own logged incidents. The shipped defaults are expert judgment, not empirically calibrated — and the reports say so.

3 · Decide

Fixed rules turn the picture into one of four verdicts: an assessed mandatory control that failed is No-Go; a mandatory control nobody has assessed yet is Insufficient Evidence — blocked, but for a different reason; three or more observed high-severity findings is Conditional; otherwise Go.
